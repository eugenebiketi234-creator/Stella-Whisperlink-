1 · Bluetooth (Web Bluetooth)

Important distinction up front: Web Bluetooth talks to BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy) devices via GATT services — it is NOT how classic Bluetooth audio (A2DP, what earbuds actually use for audio) is exposed to a web page. This section exists to confirm that directly, not because it's expected to find your earbuds as an audio device.
